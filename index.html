III

The four standing measures

Zero to ten, rated where you honestly are rather than where you would like to be seen. Anything at five or below gets flagged in the synthesis, and the answer to what one point higher looks like is what we actually discuss. The slope across cycles matters more than the level in any one.

IV

How a cycle runs

You write. Answer the form in one sitting if you can. Aim for five points where it asks for five, since the fifth is usually the one worth discussing.

You send. Finish and send produces two files. The .json goes to Chris for the synthesis. The .md is your readable copy to keep.

Chris synthesizes. Responses are combined into one prep document: answers grouped by question, standing scores side by side, carry-forward status, and the themes and tensions worth naming out loud.

We discuss. The synthesis drives the conversation, and whatever gets committed to becomes the carry-forward section on next cycle's form.
