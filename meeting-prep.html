Client service

Meeting prep template

What gets pulled before a client meeting, the shape of the meeting itself, and what has to happen after. The client should leave feeling the meeting was built for them, because it was.

I

Before

Prepared the week prior, reviewed the day before
· Read the last meeting's notes in Wealthbox What was promised, what they were worried about, and anything personal worth remembering. Open with what we said we would do. Wealthbox
· Task sweep since the last meeting Every open item for this household, closed or given an honest status. Nothing ambushes us in the room. Sweep
· Portfolio snapshot pulled Allocation against target, cash level, performance, and anything that moved. Flag drift worth discussing rather than everything. Schwab
· Plan refreshed in eMoney Balances current, and the one or two planning questions this meeting should actually move. eMoney
· Life and money events scan Tax season, RMD windows, equity vesting, a business sale, a graduation. What is happening in their life this quarter that money touches? Think
· Agenda drafted and sent ahead Three items or fewer, with the client invited to add theirs. An agenda they saw first is a meeting they co-own. Send
II

During

· Their items first Whatever they brought outranks whatever we prepared. Order
· Decisions named as decisions When something is agreed, say so out loud and note who does what by when. Ambiguity in the room becomes an email chain later. Clarity
· Jump capturing, a human summarizing Notes record themselves; judgment does not. Close by replaying the commitments in one breath. Jump
III

After

Within two business days
· Notes filed to Wealthbox Decisions, context, and anything personal to remember next time. Written for the colleague who covers this household when you are out. Record
· Tasks created with owners and dates Every commitment from the room becomes a task the day of the meeting, not when someone remembers. Tasks
· Follow-up note to the client What was decided, what we are doing, what we need from them. Short enough to be read. Send
· Next meeting anchored Date set or the OnceHub link sent before the momentum fades. OnceHub

Editing this template

The steps live in meeting-prep.html and should reflect how meetings actually run here, not how a template says they should. When a step earns a document of its own, an agenda format or a review deck master, link it from the row.
