Compliance

Form ADV

What the firm tells the SEC and its clients about itself. Current versions, what goes to whom, and when it is due. Brad owns this page and the decisions behind it; everything here is the operational summary rather than the authority.

I

Current filings

· Part 1A The structured filing on IAPD: ownership, assets under management, disciplinary history, business practices. Firm CRD 173138. IAPD · Part 2A, the brochure The plain-English narrative clients actually read. Services, fees, conflicts, discipline. IAPD's brochure page for the firm. IAPD
· Part 2B, brochure supplements One per person who gives advice. Background, education, outside business activities. Not public on IAPD; link the internal copies when they land in records/. Link not set
· Form CRS The two-page relationship summary. On the firm's IAPD page under Part 3 Relationship Summary. IAPD
· Summary of material changes What changed since the last annual update, and the version that went to clients. Link not set
II

What is due when

· Annual updating amendment Filed through IARD within 90 days of fiscal year end. Reaffirms eligibility and updates anything no longer accurate. Filed even in a year when nothing changed. 90 days
· Brochure delivery to clients Within 120 days of fiscal year end, each client gets either the updated brochure with a summary of material changes, or that summary alone plus an offer to provide the brochure. 120 days
· Interim amendments Promptly, whenever information becomes materially inaccurate. Ownership changes, new conflicts, a new website. Not something that waits for the annual cycle. Promptly
· New clients The brochure goes out at or before the advisory agreement is entered into, not after. At onboarding
· Part 2B is not filed Supplements are delivered to clients and kept available for examination rather than filed with the SEC. Keeping them current is still on us. Note

Dates run from the firm's fiscal year end. On a December year end that puts the filing at the end of March and client delivery at the end of April. Confirm the current year's exact dates with Brad rather than counting off this page.

III

The public record

· Investment Adviser Public Disclosure Where clients and prospects see the filing. Worth looking at what they see before they ask about it. Open ↗
· Firm CRD number Add it here so nobody has to search for the firm by name. Not set
· IARD credentials In the password manager, not here. Password manager
IV

Delivery record

Evidence of delivery is the part that gets examined

Who received which version, when, and how. The filing itself is easy to prove; delivery is the part firms get caught short on. Point this at wherever that log lives, whether that is the CRM, a mail-merge record, or a folder of confirmations.

·

When something changes

· Anything client-facing goes past Brad first Website copy, fee language, and service descriptions all have to match what the ADV says. Regulators read them side by side. Rule
· New advisory person A new 2B supplement, and Part 1A may need updating too. Trigger
· New service, fee, or conflict Usually a 2A change, and often material enough to need an interim amendment. Trigger
