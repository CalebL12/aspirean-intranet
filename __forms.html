Form blueprint

Netlify detects forms by reading static HTML at deploy time, so the fields for the Moving Future form, time off requests, and expense reports are declared here. The form people actually fill in is moving-future-intake.html, which posts to this page over fetch. Nothing on this page needs to be visited or edited unless the field list changes.

Field order matters: person is the first text input, so it becomes the submission title in the Netlify UI, and readable is the first textarea, so it becomes the body.
