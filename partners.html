Strategic partners

Who we work with, and the specific person to call rather than a general support line. A relationship that only one of us knows how to reach is a relationship the firm does not really have. Each partner has its own address, so partners.html#aqr links straight to that section.

· Named people, not inboxes A support address belongs in the notes. The point of this page is the person who picks up. Rule
· Coverage changes often Relationship managers rotate. Check this page after any partner reorganisation rather than finding out mid-issue. Habit
