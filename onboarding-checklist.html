Onboarding checklist

A new relationship, from signed agreement to settled routine. The order below is the general shape; the firm-specific detail in each step lives in this file and should be sharpened every time an onboarding teaches us something.

I

Before anything moves

Agreement and disclosures
· Advisory agreement signed Fee schedule attached and matching what was discussed. No account opens before this exists. Gate
· ADV brochure, supplements, and Form CRS delivered At or before the agreement is entered into, and the delivery is logged, since the log is what gets examined. Compliance
· Privacy notice delivered Initial notice at account opening. Compliance
· Household created in Wealthbox Members, entities, and how they found us. The CRM record starts on day one, not after the first meeting. Record
II

Accounts and money movement

· Custodial accounts opened at Schwab Registration matches the estate plan, not just the old accounts. Beneficiaries confirmed in writing. Schwab
· Transfers initiated and tracked In-kind where possible, with cost basis confirmed after arrival. Note anything that cannot transfer and what happens to it instead. Track
· Standing instructions and cash needs Bank links, recurring distributions, and the cash reserve level agreed with the client. Setup
· Embedded gains reviewed before anything is sold The transition plan to the target allocation is a tax decision first and a trade second. Judgment
III

Planning foundation

· eMoney profile built and portal invitation sent Accounts linked, documents vaulted, and the client shown how to see it. eMoney
· Tax return collected and read in Holistiplan Most recent return. The observations feed the first real planning conversation. Holistiplan
· Estate documents collected and summarized What exists, what is stale, and what is missing. EncorEstate for design work when it is warranted. Estate
· Insurance inventory Life, disability, umbrella, and property. Coverage against exposure, not against a product list. Review
IV

First ninety days

· Welcome and expectations note sent Who does what on the team, how to reach us, and what happens next, in writing. Send
· Thirty-day check-in Transfers reconciled, portal working, first questions surfaced. Short and scheduled, not left to chance. Day 30
· First planning meeting held The plan presented is the reason they hired us. Everything above exists so this meeting lands. Day 60
· Ninety-day review of the onboarding itself What dragged, what confused the client, and what this page should say differently next time. Day 90

Where the firm-specific detail goes

Timelines, who owns each step, and the exact document set are edited directly in onboarding-checklist.html. When a step needs a form or a template, link it from the row so the checklist is also the map.
